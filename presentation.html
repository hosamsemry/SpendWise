The Market Problem

Why Traditional Budgeting Apps Fail

Most personal finance apps suffer from three fatal friction points that cause users to abandon them within 14 days.

1

The "1st of the Month" Fallacy

Conventional apps force budgets into 1st-to-31st calendar windows. In reality, salaries arrive on the 25th, leaving end-of-month finances completely disjointed from real liquidity.

2

High Entry Friction

Logging a single espresso requires 5 taps, category dropdowns, account pickers, and date confirmations. If logging an expense takes longer than 3 seconds, daily habits break.

3

Cloud Bloat & Privacy Loss

Forced cloud signups, third-party analytics trackers, bank credentials sharing, and 150MB+ download payloads turn simple ledgers into privacy liabilities.

The Solution

The SpendWise Architectural Philosophy

Built from first principles for instant daily logging, complete privacy, and exact mathematical precision.

🗓️

Discrete Payday Cycles

Native support for custom start days (e.g. 25th) and standard 1st. Automatic clamping for February and short months (28/29/30/31 days) with leap year immunity.

⚡

100% Offline Local SQLite

Drift ORM with encrypted on-device SQLite database. Zero external servers, zero background analytics, instant launch and works seamlessly in airplane mode.

🎯

Exact Integer Minor Units

Eliminates IEEE-754 floating-point rounding errors by performing all financial arithmetic in 64-bit integer minor units (piastres / cents) with an embedded BODMAS calculator.

🏦

Dynamic Savings Vault

Automatically captures unspent surplus at cycle closeout, sweeping it into a persistent vault, and routes unallocated income straight to Savings.

Speed & UX

1-Tap Daily Quick Presets & Soft Undo

Reduce transaction entry time to less than 1 second directly from the home screen.

⚡ Direct Dashboard Chips

Users tap customizable preset chips (e.g. "Espresso • 45 EGP", "Metro • 10 EGP", "Salary") to record transactions instantly without opening input dialogs.

↺ Non-Intrusive Soft Undo

Every quick preset triggers a floating SnackBar with an Undo button that auto-dismisses after 3 seconds, enabling instant recovery from accidental taps.

🔔 Real-Time Threshold Checks

Presets evaluate category budget thresholds upon tap, alerting users if an entry pushes them past 80% or 100% of budgeted limits.

Financial Awareness

Daily Spending Clarity & Dynamic Safe Pace

Prevent month-end deficits through proactive daily pacing instead of reactive end-of-month regret.

📊 The Dynamic Safe Pace Algorithm

SpendWise computes a daily spending allowance that adapts dynamically as you log expenses or days pass:

Safe Daily Pace = Net Remaining Budget / Days Remaining in Cycle

Calculated using timezone-normalized UTC arithmetic to eliminate daylight saving time (DST) day-drift errors.

Flagship Feature

The Savings Vault & Surplus Sweeper

Transform unspent budget into accumulated wealth automatically at the end of each cycle.

🧹

Surplus Sweep Banner

When a budget cycle concludes with positive unused balance, a prominent banner appears on the dashboard offering a 1-tap sweep into your Savings Vault.

💎

Persistent Vault Balance

The vault accumulates swept surplus across all historical cycles. Supports manual deposits and emergency withdrawals with overdraft protection.

⚖️

Unallocated Income Sync

When income exceeds assigned category allocations, SpendWise automatically syncs and rebalances the difference into a dedicated Savings category.

Privacy & Portability

Bank-Grade Security & Open Portability

Complete user ownership with hardware-backed security and zero vendor lock-in.

🔒 Cryptographic SHA-256 PIN & Biometrics

Local salted PIN hashing with fingerprint/face biometric unlock. Includes failed-attempt throttling (30-second lockout) and intelligent lifecycle sensing:

• Swiping status bar (inactive): No lock, zero interruption
• Leaving app / screen lock (paused): Locks immediately

💾 Offline JSON Backup & CSV Export

Full data portability without cloud servers:

• Full JSON Snapshot: Exports complete SQLite state with schema validation.
• PIN Preservation: Choice to keep current PIN or overwrite during restore.
• Clean Decimal CSV: Unformatted numbers without comma splits for Excel.
