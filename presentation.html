The Problem

Why Traditional Money Trackers Fail

Users abandon financial apps within 7 days because of friction, clutter, and misalignment with real financial life.

1

Painful Entry Friction

Logging a cup of coffee requires opening 4 nested dialogs, selecting dates, picking tags, and tapping confirm. People quit logging when entry takes more than 3 seconds.

2

The "1st of the Month" Myth

Standard apps tie budgets to calendar months (1st to 30th). But the majority of working professionals receive salaries on the 25th, making calendar-month budgeting inaccurate and frustrating.

3

Cloud Bloat & Privacy Risks

Forced cloud signups, intrusive analytics, bank account linking, and oversized app bundles (200MB+) that drain battery and put sensitive financial data on third-party servers.

Product Philosophy

Designed for Daily Speed & Clarity

Every architectural decision was made to keep the app lightweight, private, and effortless.

📅

Realistic 25th-to-24th Cycles

Budgets automatically align with your actual payday cycle. Automatically transitions every 25th with rollover notifications, safe-to-spend daily pace metrics, and zero manual cycle resets.

⚡

Zero Cloud, Zero Waiting

100% on-device SQLite database powered by Drift. Instant queries, instant inserts, works in airplane mode, zero registration, and complete privacy of your financial records.

🏷️

Separated Expense & Income Taxonomies

No confusing merged categories. Expense categories carry monthly budget limits, while Income categories track salary and revenue with custom default expectations.

🪶

Ultra Lightweight & Fast

Optimized APK size under 80MB. Clean minimal Material Design 3 with high-contrast Dark Mode tokens, smooth 60fps animations, and zero heavy third-party tracking SDKs.

Frictionless UX

1-Tap Direct Quick Presets

Record recurring expenses directly from the home screen in under one second.

Direct Ledger Insertion

Tapping a preset chip (e.g., "Morning Espresso • 45.00 EGP") instantly saves the transaction to the database without presenting any modal dialogs.

3-Second Auto-Dismissing Undo

Displays a sleek floating confirmation card with an Undo button. If tapped accidentally, one touch reverses the insertion; otherwise, it slides away after 3 seconds.

Real-Time Threshold Monitoring

Even with 1-tap presets, the app continuously monitors budget limits, notifying users if a quick preset pushes a category near or over its allocated budget.

Privacy & Security

Bank-Grade Privacy & Smart PIN Lock

Financial privacy without frustrating usability interruptions.

🔒

Cryptographic SHA-256 PIN Lock

Your 4-digit PIN is salted and hashed using standard SHA-256 before storing locally in SQLite. The raw PIN never touches disk, memory leaks, or cloud endpoints.

📱

Smart Lifecycle State Awareness

Unlike crude PIN locks that trigger on any screen touch, SpendWise understands Android/iOS lifecycle states:

  • Swiping status bar / Wi-Fi toggle: App remains unlocked. Zero annoying interruptions.
  • Leaving app or locking screen: Enters background paused state; immediately locks your session.
⏰

Daily Evening Reminder Notification

Local background notification scheduled at your chosen time (default 9:00 PM). Automatically detects device IANA time zone (e.g. Africa/Cairo) and uses Android exact alarms.

📁

Full CSV Data Ownership

1-tap export to standard CSV. Export all transactions or current cycle summary to Excel, Google Sheets, or WhatsApp. You own 100% of your data.
